# LAUNCH.md

One page. Where to post, in what order, what to do with replies. The messages themselves are in `launch/messages.md`. The landing page copy is live in the app (`src/pages/Landing.tsx`) and reproduced in `launch/landing-copy.md`.

## Before day 1 (all in HUMAN.md)

- Firebase project live, app deployed, `VITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL` pointing at an inbox you read.
- Sign in yourself, add a fake student, send yourself the parent link, tick a day. If that takes more than two minutes, fix it before anyone else sees it.

## The order

**Week 1: Newcastle first, by name.**
1. Email the MTA NSW Newcastle branch (`newcastle@mtansw.org.au`) with message M1. Ask for two things: to try it with a couple of teachers, and to mention it once to members.
2. Find 15 Newcastle/Lake Macquarie piano teachers from musicteacher.com.au (90 listed) and their own websites or Facebook pages. Send message M2 to each, personalised with one true detail (suburb, an exam result they posted, an instrument). Five a day, three days. Track them in `launch/outreach-tracker.csv`.
3. Ask your own past or current music teacher, if you have one, to try it. That is customer zero.

**Week 1 in parallel: reply where the complaint lives.**
4. The Reddit threads in VALIDATION.md are mostly archived. For each poster still active, send message M3 as a DM. Five a day. Do not paste a link into old threads.
5. Join r/pianoteachers, r/piano, and two Facebook groups (Piano Teachers PLUS; an Australian piano teachers group). Read the rules. For seven days, answer other people's questions with no mention of the product.

**Week 2: the one post per community.**
6. Post P1 in r/pianoteachers (check the self-promotion rule; if it forbids, ask a mod first with message M4).
7. Post P2 in the two Facebook groups, two days apart.
8. Post P3 in the Piano Pantry / Vibrant Music Teaching communities if they have an open forum.

**Week 3 onward: repeat what worked, drop what didn't.**
9. Whichever channel produced two or more real trials gets all the attention for the next month. Everything else stops.
10. Ask every teacher who has sent three or more slips for one sentence of feedback and permission to quote it on the landing page.

## What to do with replies

- **Reply within 24 hours, every time.** Speed is the whole advantage of being one person.
- **"Interested" → send the sign-in link and R1.** Offer a ten-minute screen-share the same week. Most won't take it; the offer signals you're real.
- **"How much / who are you / is it safe" → R2.** Honest, short. Age is not a secret; it's part of the story.
- **Any teacher who signs up → note it in the admin dashboard, then on day 7 send R3** (the Mom Test questions, three of them, in an email). Write down what they say in `launch/interviews.md`. This is the most valuable thing you'll collect.
- **Day 21 of a trial → R4**, the invoice ask, only if they've sent at least two slips. If they haven't, ask why instead (R5).
- **Any complaint or bug → fix or explain within a day, then reply with what changed.** Then log it in the feedback panel so the weekly loop sees it.
- **Silence → one follow-up after five days (R6), then stop.**

## Numbers that mean it's working

- 10 teachers who have sent a slip to a real parent within 30 days of launch.
- 5 of them still sending slips in week 4.
- 3 paid invoices within 60 days.

If week 4 has fewer than 3 teachers still sending slips, stop posting and go back to conversations. The problem is the product or the pitch, not the reach.

## Numbers that mean it isn't

- 30 sign-ups and fewer than 5 slips sent: teachers are curious but the writing step is too slow or unclear. Watch someone do it.
- Slips sent but parents never open: the delivery step (teacher's own messaging) is failing. Ask how they sent it.
- Parents open but never tick: the parent doesn't see why. Revisit the copy on the parent page.
