**What we store**

- For teachers: your email address, the name you enter, an optional studio name, and when you signed up. If you subscribe, the period you've paid for. No card details ever touch our systems.
- For each student a teacher adds: a first name, an instrument, an optional parent first name, the practice items the teacher writes, an optional note from the parent, and which days were ticked. We don't ask for surnames, ages, emails or phone numbers of students or parents, and we don't collect them from the parent page.
- Usage events: that a slip was written, sent, opened or ticked, with a timestamp. Used to see whether the product works. Not sold, not shared.
- Feedback you type into the Feedback box.

**Where it lives**

In Google Firebase (Firestore and Hosting), which is how most small web apps are built. Google's own security and privacy terms apply to that storage.

**The parent link**

Each student has a private link made of a random code. Anyone with the link can see that student's slip and tick days. Treat it like any private link: send it only to the parent. We can't see who opens it beyond a count.

**Deleting**

Delete a student in the app and everything about them is removed immediately. To delete your whole teacher account and all students, email us from your sign-in address; it's done within a day.

**Cookies and tracking**

None beyond what's needed to keep you signed in. No advertising trackers, no analytics scripts from third parties.

**Children**

The product is used by adults (teachers and parents). Children don't have accounts and aren't asked to enter anything.
