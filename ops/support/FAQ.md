# FAQ — the answers the responder may send

Personalise the first line. Keep the rest as written; it's been checked against how the product actually behaves.

### How do I send the slip to a parent?
On the student's page, tap **Send to parent**. On a phone it opens your share sheet (WhatsApp, Messages, email, whatever you use) with the slip text and the link already written. On a computer it copies the text so you can paste it anywhere. The link is permanent for that student: send it once and every new slip appears at the same address, so most teachers only send it the first week.

### The parent says the link doesn't work / "This link isn't active"
That message appears only if the student was deleted, or the link was copied incompletely. Ask them to open it from your original message rather than retyping it, and check the student still exists on your Students page. If it's still failing, send me the link (it's just a random code, nothing private) and I'll check it.

### Does the parent need an account or an app?
No. The link opens in any browser. No login, no install, nothing to remember except bookmarking the page.

### Do I need to write a new slip every week?
Only when something changes. **New slip from last week** copies everything across so it's usually a 20-second edit. Each slip runs for seven days from the day you write it.

### What does the child see?
Nothing, unless the parent shows them. Practice Slip is written for the parent. There are no points, streaks or leaderboards on purpose.

### Can I print it?
Yes. **Print** on the student's page gives a one-page version with the seven circles to tick by hand.

### How much is it, and how do I pay?
$9 AUD a month or $79 a year, per teacher, unlimited students. The first 30 days are free with everything unlocked. To keep going: Billing → Request invoice. You'll get an invoice to pay by bank transfer or PayID; your account switches on the day it's paid. Card payments are coming.

### My trial ended. Is my stuff gone?
No. Students, slips and parent links all stay exactly as they were, and parents can keep ticking. You just can't write new slips until you subscribe.

### I didn't get the sign-in email
Check spam for "Practice Slip". The link is one-use and must be opened on the same device you requested it from. Request another from the sign-in page; the old one stops working. If you're on Gmail, "Continue with Google" is faster.

### What do you store, and can I delete it?
For each student: a first name, the instrument, the parent's first name if you typed one, the practice items you wrote, and which days were ticked. No parent email addresses, no child accounts. Delete a student and it's gone immediately. To delete your whole account, reply from your sign-in email and I'll do it the same day.

### Can two parents use the link?
Yes. Send the same link to both; they'll see the same slip and the same ticks.
