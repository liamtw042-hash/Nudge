# OFFER.md

## One sentence

**Practice Slip** turns the practice notes you already write at the end of a lesson into a link the parent opens on their phone, ticks off each day the child practises, and you see who practised before they walk in.

## Who it's for

Private piano and instrument teachers with a home studio, teaching children whose parents drive practice.

## Price

- **$9 AUD / month** or **$79 AUD / year** (two months free), per teacher, unlimited students, unlimited parents.
- 30-day free trial, everything unlocked, no card. Trial ends in a soft lock: you can still see everything, you just can't write next week's slip until you subscribe.
- First customers pay by invoice (bank transfer or PayID). Card checkout follows once a merchant-of-record account exists (see HUMAN.md).

Positioned under every competitor (Practice Space $15.99, My Music Staff $16.95, Vivid $9–29, Practis $9.99–39.99) because there is no student app to build or maintain, and because the buyer is a sole trader who feels $16/month.

## Why someone pays (value equation)

- **Dream outcome:** students turn up having practised; the parent knows exactly what "practise" meant this week; fewer lessons wasted re-teaching.
- **Likelihood:** high, because it changes nothing the teacher does (they already write the assignment) and asks the parent for one tap, not an app, an account or a password.
- **Time to result:** the first slip takes under a minute to write and is in a parent's hands before the child has put their shoes on.
- **Effort:** near zero for the teacher; zero install for parent or child. The link works in WhatsApp, iMessage, email, whatever the studio already uses.

## The stack of the offer

1. The weekly practice slip: pieces, what to do, target days, a note to the parent. "Copy last week" so a slip is usually a 20-second edit.
2. The parent link: one permanent link per student. Opens instantly, no login. Tap a day, done. Optional one-line note back to the teacher.
3. The teacher's Monday view: every student, days practised this week, who's gone quiet.
4. Print view: for the families who want paper, the same slip prints on one page.
5. Guarantee: if your students aren't practising more after 30 days, don't subscribe. Nothing to cancel because nothing was charged.

## What it is not

No points, streaks, leaderboards or badges. No student app. No scheduling, invoicing or lesson booking (My Music Staff and TutorBird do that well). One job: make the week between lessons visible to the parent and the teacher.

## The line to say out loud

"It's the practice notebook, except the parent actually sees it."
