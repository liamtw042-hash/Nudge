## P1 — Reddit post, r/pianoteachers

**Title:** I built a practice slip that goes to the parent, not the student. Looking for 5 teachers to tell me what's wrong with it.

I'm 15, in Australia, and I've read a lot of threads here about students not practising and parents not being in the loop. The pattern I kept seeing: the fix always runs through the parent (signed charts, conversations at the door), but every app is built for the kid's device, with points and streaks the kid stops caring about by week three, and portals parents "really don't" open.

So I built the opposite. Practice Slip:

- You write this week's practice at the end of the lesson. It copies last week's, so it's usually a 20-second edit.
- It becomes a link. You send it to the parent from your own phone, in whatever you already use (WhatsApp, text, email).
- The parent opens it. No app, no login. They see the pieces, what to do, and seven circles. They tap a circle each day the child practises. One tap.
- You see who practised, and who's gone quiet since Wednesday, before they walk in.
- No points, no streaks, no leaderboards. It prints on one page for paper families.

It's free for 30 days and then $9 AUD a month if it earns it. I'm not looking for sign-ups so much as five teachers who'll use it for two weeks and tell me bluntly what's wrong. Link in a comment (or DM me if the sub prefers).

If you've tried Practice Space, Vivid, Better Practice or Tonara back in the day, I'd especially like to hear what did and didn't stick with your parents.
