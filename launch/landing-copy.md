# Landing page copy (as shipped in `src/pages/Landing.tsx`)

**Eyebrow:** For private music teachers

**Headline:** The practice notebook, except the parent actually sees it.

**Lede:** Write this week's practice in under a minute at the end of the lesson. It becomes a link. Send it to the parent from your own phone. They tick each day the child practises. You see who practised before they walk in.

**Primary button:** Start free for 30 days
**Under the button:** No card. No app for the child to install.

**Demo slip:** Ava's practice this week. Minuet in G (hands together, bars 1–8, slowly, three times, no mistakes). C major scale (two octaves, hands separately, say the finger numbers). Sight-reading card 12 (clap it first). Note: "Short and often beats one long session. Five minutes on a school night is a win." Seven circles, four ticked.

**Four features:**
- Under a minute. Copy last week's slip, change what changed, done. Templates for the things you assign every week.
- No app, no login. The parent opens a link. That's it. It works in WhatsApp, iMessage, email, whatever your studio already uses.
- Know before the lesson. One screen on Monday: every student, days practised, who's gone quiet since Wednesday.
- No points, no streaks. Nothing for the child to game and nothing to nag about. A slip and seven circles. Prints on one page too.

**Price block:** $9 AUD a month, or $79 a year. Per teacher. Unlimited students, unlimited parents. 30 days free first, nothing charged until you decide.

**Why this and not a practice app:** Practice apps are built for the child's device: timers, points, streaks, leaderboards. Teachers of young students keep saying the same thing: the parent is the lever. Portals parents have to log into don't get opened. So this one asks the parent for one tap, on a link they already have, and asks the child for nothing.

**Footer line:** Built in Newcastle, NSW. Questions go to a person, not a bot.

## Copy rules

- Plain, specific, a little dry. No exclamation marks.
- Every claim is something the product literally does.
- "Parent" not "families". "Slip" not "assignment". "Tick" not "log".
- Say what it isn't (no points, no app) as often as what it is; that's the positioning.
